# Glance

Glance is the software that runs on your Glance Display, a small desk screen that shows what you care about at a glance: the time, the weather, prayer times, your parcels and more. It also warns you about things like earthquakes and rain.

Meet Pico, the little coral owl who keeps you company when a screen has nothing to show.

## What it shows

- **Clock and weather**: the time, the date and the weather for your city.
- **Calendar**: the month, with today ringed.
- **Prayer times**: worked out from where you live, with a Ramadan mode for suhoor and iftar.
- **Claude usage**: how much of your Claude Code limits you have used, and when they reset.
- **PC temps**: how hot your computer is and how busy it is.
- **Parcels**: where your deliveries are, with a screen of their own for the one you care about most.
- **Alerts**: earthquakes, storms, floods and "rain soon" fill the whole screen for a short while, then go away by themselves.

Press the button on the Glance Display to move to the next screen, or let it rotate through them on its own.

## Getting started

1. Plug in your Glance Display. The first time, it shows **Setup mode**.
2. On your phone or computer, join the WiFi network called **Glance-Setup**.
3. A page opens. Choose your home WiFi, type its password and save. (The Glance Display works with 2.4 GHz WiFi.)
4. The Glance Display shows an address on its screen. Open that address in any web browser on the same WiFi to change settings and set your city.

If the clock says "Syncing time..." for more than a minute, check that your WiFi has internet.

## Glance Manager

Glance Manager is a free app for your computer. It is optional, but it unlocks the screens that need your computer's help: Claude usage, PC temps, parcels and earthquake alerts. It also gives you all the settings in one place, including one-tap themes. Install it, leave it running in the tray, and it takes care of the rest.

## Settings

Open the address shown on the Glance Display in a browser, or use Glance Manager. You can:

- Pick a **theme**: Clay, Ocean, Forest, Amoled or Mono. One tap recolors every screen.
- Choose your **city**, units and time zone. Glance can also find your city by itself.
- Choose which **prayer layout** you like, and turn on Ramadan mode.
- Decide what you want to be **alerted** about, how close it must be, and set quiet hours.
- Set the **brightness** and choose which screens appear when it rotates.
- Change the **colors** of the clock screen, piece by piece.

Every group of settings has a reset button, so you can always go back to how it came.

## Updating

New versions are installed over WiFi, and your settings are kept.

1. Open the Glance Display's address in a browser and add `/update` at the end.
2. Choose the new Glance file and upload it. Keep the display plugged in.
3. It restarts by itself after about half a minute.

You can also update from Glance Manager under Device > System.

## Help

- **The screen is black after an update:** open the settings page and press Save once.
- **The weather says "Loading" for a long time:** check that the display has internet, then press "Refresh weather". Check how you spelled your city.
- **The time is wrong:** set your city or pick a time zone by hand.
- **Prayer times are a minute or two off:** adjust them on the Prayer tab, or let Glance Manager compare several sources for you.
- **A faint shadow of another screen is visible:** this can happen on any screen that shows the same picture for a long time. Use Display > Clear ghosting.
- **Claude, PC, parcel or alert screens are empty:** Glance Manager needs to be running on your computer, on the same WiFi.
- **The display says SAFE MODE:** it restarted after a problem. Unplug it for a few seconds. If it comes back, update the firmware again.
- **You lost the address:** hold the button for about 1.5 seconds. The address shows for 20 seconds.
- **You changed your WiFi or moved house:** open the settings, go to WiFi and forget the network. The display opens **Glance-Setup** again.

## Privacy

Glance only goes online for the weather, your location (if you let it find it), the time, and the earthquake and disaster feeds that Glance Manager fetches for you. Your Claude usage, PC temperatures and parcels stay on your own network.

The settings page has no password, so anyone on your WiFi can open it. Do not make the Glance Display reachable from the internet.
